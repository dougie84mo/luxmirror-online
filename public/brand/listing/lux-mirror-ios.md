## Description — 1,486/4,000

```
LUX Mirror is the business app for salons and barbershops running on the LUX Smart Mirror — and it works just as well before your first mirror arrives.

RUN THE DAY
See every chair at a glance. Today's appointments, who's in, what's next, and what changed since you last looked.

BOOKINGS THAT FIT YOUR SHOP
Set each stylist's hours, services, buffers, and time off, and let the calendar do the rest. Clients book the slots you actually have.

CLIENTS WORTH REMEMBERING
Formulas, notes, history, and photos from the chair, kept with the person they belong to. Your team walks in already knowing.

YOUR TEAM, YOUR RULES
Invite stylists and managers with the access each one needs. Owners keep billing and devices to themselves.

YOUR MIRRORS, LIVE
Pair a LUX Smart Mirror by scanning the code on its screen. From then on you can see every unit's status from anywhere, and unpair one in a tap.

BUILT FOR MORE THAN ONE CHAIR
Multiple locations, multiple stylists, one account. Plans size from a single chair to a multi-shop fleet.

WHAT IT COSTS
LUX Mirror is free to download. Cloud features run on a LUX subscription, billed monthly and cancellable any time. Details at theluxmirror.com/pricing.

A LUX Smart Mirror is optional — the booking, client, and team tools work on their own. Mirrors ship Q4 2026.

Privacy: theluxmirror.com/privacy
Terms: theluxmirror.com/terms
```
