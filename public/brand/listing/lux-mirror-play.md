```
LUX Mirror is the business app for salons and barbershops. Run your bookings, your clients, your team, and your LUX Smart Mirrors from one place — on the floor or from home.

RUN THE DAY
Today's appointments, who's working, what's next. Open the app and know where the shop stands.

BOOKINGS THAT FIT YOUR SHOP
Set each stylist's hours, services, buffers, and time off. Clients only ever see the slots you actually have, and double-bookings stop being a thing.

CLIENTS WORTH REMEMBERING
Colour formulas, notes, visit history, and photos taken at the chair — all kept with the client they belong to. Whoever takes the appointment walks in already knowing.

YOUR TEAM, YOUR RULES
Invite stylists and managers with the access each one needs. Owners keep billing and device management to themselves.

YOUR MIRRORS, LIVE
Pair a LUX Smart Mirror by scanning the code on its screen. See every unit's status from anywhere, and unpair one in a tap when a chair moves.

BUILT FOR MORE THAN ONE CHAIR
Multiple locations, multiple stylists, one account. Plans size from a single chair to a multi-shop fleet.

WHAT IT COSTS
Free to download. Cloud features run on a LUX subscription, billed monthly and cancellable any time — see theluxmirror.com/pricing.

You do not need a LUX Smart Mirror to use this app. Booking, client, and team tools work on their own. Mirrors ship Q4 2026.

Privacy policy: https://theluxmirror.com/privacy
Terms of service: https://theluxmirror.com/terms
Support: support@theluxmirror.com
```
