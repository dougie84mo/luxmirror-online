## Description — 1,297/4,000

```
LUX Booking is how you book a chair at a shop running LUX — and how you keep track of what they did last time.

FIND A SHOP
Search by what you need, or see what's near you. Real availability from real calendars, not a request form that might get answered.

BOOK IN A FEW TAPS
Pick a service, pick your stylist — or take the first one free — and choose a time that actually exists. Confirmation is instant.

NO MORE "WHAT DID WE DO LAST TIME"
Every visit is on your timeline, with the photos your stylist took at the chair. Show up, show the picture, done.

RUNNING LATE, OR NOT COMING
Reschedule or cancel from your phone. Your shop sees it immediately, and someone else gets the slot.

CHECK IN FROM THE DOOR
Let the shop know you've arrived without interrupting the person in the chair.

REMINDERS THAT ACTUALLY HELP
A nudge before your appointment, and a note when something changes. That's it — we don't spam.

LUX Booking is free. There's nothing to buy in the app; you pay your shop the way you always have.

Privacy: theluxmirror.com/privacy
```

## Review notes

- **Demo account:** `<email>` / `<password>` — has upcoming and past bookings and
  a photo library so every screen has content. **Required**; the app is

  optional — decline it and search still works by name. Say this explicitly;
  reviewers flag unexplained location prompts.
